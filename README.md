### 🔬 Research — Final Year Design Project

**Beyond Sentiment: Detecting Emotional Distress and Support-Seeking Behavior in Bangla Social Media Using Multi-Task Learning**

Final Year Design Project I · Southeast University

Sentiment analysis can only say whether a post is positive, negative or neutral — but a post expressing emotional distress and a post asking for help can both look simply "negative". Our research goes beyond sentiment: we are building a **multi-task learning** model (one shared text encoder with task-specific classification heads) that detects **emotional distress** and **support-seeking behaviour** as two separate signals in Bangla, English and Banglish (code-mixed) social media posts. Our dataset is annotated with five label groups — sentiment, emotional distress, support-seeking, depression risk and emotion — and we compare **BanglaBERT** and **XLM-RoBERTa** using accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score. This is a research prototype only, not a clinical or diagnostic tool.

**Keywords:** Bangla NLP · Multi-Task Learning · BanglaBERT · XLM-RoBERTa · Sentiment Analysis · Affective Computing
